I am a Master's student in Health System Research at the University of Toronto, supervised by <a href="https://www.uhnresearch.ca/researcher/quynh-pham">Quynh Pham</a>, with <a href="https://ihpme.utoronto.ca/faculty-profile/velmovitsky-pedro/">Pedro Velmovitsky</a> and <a href="https://ihpme.utoronto.ca/faculty-profile/abad-shakeri-hossain-zahra/">Zahra Shakeri</a> on my thesis committee. My research focuses on evaluating large language models (LLMs) in clinical settings, with an emphasis on benchmarking LLM-powered tools for chronic disease management.

I am also a research trainee at the <a href="https://www.centrefordigitaltherapeutics.ca/">Centre for Digital Therapeutics</a> at the University Health Network (UHN) and at the Centre for Addiction and Mental Health (CAMH) through the <a href="https://www.camh.ca/500.html?aspxerrorpath=/en/science-and-research/institutes-and-centres/the-margaret-and-wallace-mccain-centre-for-child-youth-and-family-mental-health">McCain Centre for Child, Youth & Family Mental Health Research</a>.

Prior to graduate school, I spent over seven years in the technology industry. Most recently, I led a global team of data scientists at <a href="https://www.uber.com/">Uber</a> within the Measurement Science team. Earlier in my career, I was an entrepreneur and built products aimed at addressing youth mental health.
