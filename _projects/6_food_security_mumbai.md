**Institution**: Global Ideas Institute, Munk School of Global Affairs & Public Policy, University of Toronto

**Timeline**: September 2012 – April 2016

## Objective

Examine barriers to equitable access to nutrition for children in Mumbai and explore scalable distribution models for nutritious food packages.

## Outcome

- **Nutrition Source Analysis**: Analyzed sources of nutrition for youth in Mumbai to identify gaps in access to nutritious food
- **Supply Chain Mapping**: Mapped the distribution and supply chain of nutritional products to better understand systemic gaps and barriers
- **Scalable Solution Design**: Collaborated with a multidisciplinary team to propose scalable solutions, including distributing nutrition packages through vending machines
- **Stakeholder Engagement**: Presented research findings and proposed solutions to faculty members and stakeholders at the Munk School of Global Affairs

## Key Contributions

- Conducted field research in Mumbai to understand barriers to child nutrition
- Developed supply chain analysis to identify bottlenecks in food distribution
- Proposed innovative distribution models using technology (vending machines) to improve accessibility
- Collaborated across disciplines to design practical, scalable interventions
