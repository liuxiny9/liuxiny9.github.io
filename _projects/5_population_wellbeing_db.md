**Institution**: University of Toronto, Population Well-Being Lab, Department of Psychology

**Timeline**: September 2023 – March 2024

## Problem Statement

Sourcing, cleaning, and organizing secondary data sources can be time-consuming and inefficient. This project aimed to design, implement, and maintain a centralized Population Well-Being database to help researchers efficiently retrieve, manage, and organize datasets.

## Outcome

- **User Research**: Interviewed researchers to identify pain points in accessing and using secondary data sources
- **Database Design**: Designed the Entity-Relationship Diagram (ERD) for the centralized database system
- **Process Standardization**: Developed a standardized data cleaning process playbook (completed June 2024)
- **Impact**: Improved efficiency in data retrieval and management for population well-being research

## Key Contributions

- Conducted stakeholder interviews to understand researcher needs and workflows
- Created comprehensive database architecture to centralize disparate data sources
- Established standardized protocols for data cleaning and organization
- Delivered documentation and playbook for ongoing database maintenance
