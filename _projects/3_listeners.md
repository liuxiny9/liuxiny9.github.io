**Project Status**: Ongoing

## Problem Statement

An increasing number of youth experience mental health challenges, particularly international students who face language barriers and limited access to culturally and linguistically appropriate support.

## Product

Founded and scaled a peer-support network across Canada where students in need can book one-on-one peer listening sessions (解聆) with trained peer listeners. The platform focused on accessibility, cultural sensitivity, and early, stigma-free support.

## Key Features

- **Peer-to-Peer Support**: One-on-one listening sessions connecting students with trained peer listeners
- **Cultural Sensitivity**: Culturally and linguistically appropriate support for international students
- **Accessibility**: Stigma-free, early intervention mental health support
- **Scalability**: Expanded across multiple Canadian universities

## Impact

- Scaled the peer-support network across Canada
- Provided accessible mental health support to students facing language and cultural barriers
- Reduced stigma around seeking mental health help among international student communities

## Presentation / Media Coverage

- **Lunch Box Talk**: Presented findings at Hart House, University of Toronto
- **CBC Radio**: Interviewed about international students' mental health, raising public awareness
- **Academic Coverage**: Interviewed by Ryerson University and The Varsity (University of Toronto) about research findings and program effectiveness
