Anti-Pollution Mask Design for Chinese Senior Citizens

Designing senior-friendly face masks to increase adoption during severe air pollution in China

Project Timeline: Oct 2015 – Apr 2016 Project Status: Completed

Problem Statement

Chinese senior citizens showed significant resistance to mask-wearing during periods of severe air pollution, limiting their protection during the “Great Smog” events. This project aimed to:

  • Understand the barriers to mask-wearing among Chinese senior citizens
  • Design masks that are more comfortable, wearable, and acceptable to increase adoption

Project Details

Collaborated with the Cleanopy team, led by Natalia Mykhaylova, to design and prototype senior-friendly face masks using 3D printing technology. The work focused on:

  • Ergonomic Design: Tailored fit for older adults’ facial structure
  • Comfort: Improved breathability and reduced skin irritation
  • Usability: Easy-to-use fastening mechanisms and lightweight materials
  • Cultural Acceptability: Design aesthetics that appeal to the target demographic

Key Technologies

  • 3D Printing for rapid prototyping
  • User-centered design methodology

Outcome

Presented the research findings and design solutions as a finalist in the Hatchery program sponsored by the Faculty of Applied Science and Engineering, University of Toronto.
